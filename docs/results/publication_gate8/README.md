## External anisotropic baseline feasibility

**ShinyNeRF is the strongest directly relevant released comparison for anisotropic held-out appearance and tangent direction found in this review.** Its published author implementation accepts posed NeRF-synthetic views and exposes normals/tangents and its anisotropic lobe parameters. It predicts outgoing appearance with anisotropic spherical Gaussian encoding. Its published evaluation and released interface do not establish calibrated incident-light replacement, fixed oracle visibility, or recovery of Kurt/Beckmann widths and world Q. Compare held-out L2 images and eligible tangent direction only after verifying linear-target ingestion, masks, camera conventions and tangent/lobe correspondence. Retain ShinyNeRF's native geometry and label the experiment as an appearance comparison. Its concentration and eccentricity remain in native units. [ShinyNeRF paper](https://doi.org/10.5194/isprs-archives-XLVIII-2-W12-2026-33-2026) · [Author code](https://github.com/multimedia-eurecat/ShinyNeRF)

For an external **relightable** anisotropic control, the feasible fallback is a separately specified fixed-geometry texture fit with Mitsuba 3's stock anisotropic Beckmann BSDF and known incident maps. Mitsuba exposes separate roughness parameters along the mesh UV frame. That solver would require a declared UV/tangent scheme or an orientation-aware wrapper and a documented BSDF allocation, with independently verified visibility, exposure and error metrics. It is a published rendering tool and a proposed control, without precedent here as a published end-to-end method matching the Donut acquisition. Compare directional rendering and native parameters where meaningful. Do not treat a stock conductor or dielectric as the oracle additive mixed-metallic law. [Mitsuba BSDF documentation](https://mitsuba.readthedocs.io/en/latest/src/generated/plugins_bsdfs.html)

Preserve a **NeRO schedule sensitivity** as a reviewer-facing option. Gate 7 used the common 10,000-update budget while the pinned synthetic configuration specifies 100,000 total updates with a 100,000-step schedule. A separately approved fresh fit could report both a replayed 10,000-update checkpoint and the prespecified 100,000-update endpoint, preserving the Gate 7 acquisition and sample-count controls. Report the increased compute and treat this as an optimization-budget sensitivity. The controlled Gate 7 result, its isotropic limitation and the historical archive retain their own scope.
