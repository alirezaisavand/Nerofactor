the original G `train_step`, preserves the exact foreground L1 and adds only
`mean((1-mask)*fg_acc)` before the original mask schedule. A retained pre-call
mask view handles the original shuffle boundary correctly. No extra head,
parameters, RNG draws, gradient pass or illumination change is introduced.

The native initial-state fingerprint must match archived O0 before the first
forward. Reuse the accepted split-scoped acquisition, original Trainer.run,
successful eight-worker spawn/bootstrap, executable owned extension cache and
unchanged image. One future approval covers the complete300k history, natural

The evaluation uses the accepted domain-aware raw-SDF policy and exact float32
cleanup at all four checkpoints, all122 fitting rasters, six original validation/
held rasters, FP64 witnesses and fixed16-camera/two-draw neural-opacity panel.
No repetition of O0, original mesh failure or camera-precision investigation.
The original native numerical and geometry admission criteria remain unchanged.
O1's final eligibility is a researcher-review record, not automatic admission.

The fixed300k endpoint is primary. Compare silhouette precision **and recall**,
foreground correspondence, depth and signed-normal distributions/tails, topology,
negative SDF extension, opacity and runtime. Intermediate quality cannot replace
the endpoint. A lower false-positive count does not establish better geometry if
foreground support, normals or depth deteriorate. Bell remains development data.

The source/seed/policy match is prospective and enforced; O0 did not archive every
per-update ray/RNG tape. Geometry-dependent branches can consume different GPU
random counts after divergence. Do not describe this as a measured full-history
bitwise stochastic replay. Original authoritative sources and schedules are intact.

## Realistic-mask readiness

The existing SAM2 environment meets the installed project's declared version
requirements; large-model weights/config and compiled extension are present and
hashed. No dependency installation is currently justified. Model loading,
extension compatibility, actual GPU memory and native inference remain untested.
The 128² image detail and angular changes in numerical camera order may limit
propagation quality; the prospective four-pass protocol has no demonstrated
segmentation-quality result yet.
The original confidence maps were not recovered in the previously completed
scoped search; new inference would create a new acquisition.

The [realistic acquisition and R0/R1 protocol](../../protocols/BELL_IMPERFECT_MASK_ACQUISITION_GATE11_V1.md)
records122 fitting RGBs, two manual image-only prompt sets on cameras0/127,
fixed numerical frame order and four independent forward/reverse passes. It
requires raw pre-hole-fill logits as well as official postprocessed decision
scores, with explicit original-resolution calibration and image mapping.

Conservative foreground is unchanged between R0 and R1. Background needs
four-pass agreement below−1, a four-pixel uncertainty margin and positive object
presence in every pass; otherwise abstain. Thresholds are fixed heuristics, not
probability calibration. Prompt/propagation agreement is correlated and cannot
guarantee correctness. An image-only background veto can add uncertainty before
both histories, without changing foreground or tuning against GT/held images.

Only byte metadata and two fitting RGBs were inspected. No SAM2 model was imported
or loaded, no segmentation was inferred, and no geometry/reference/model evaluation
ran. A dedicated inference adapter/freeze, bounded approval and label admission
are required before R0/R1 training. Their comparison must use identical imperfect
masks and other settings. Perfect-mask O1 success cannot establish robustness to
undersegmentation or be presented as the realistic method.

## Research priorities and contribution limits

Complete O1 first; its added background supervision is an established diagnostic,
not claimed methodological novelty. Then prepare a same-imperfect-mask R0/R1 pair
if the outcome supports a realistic intervention. Keep direct multi-view free-space
SDF, anisotropy-aware directional appearance, ambient tensor integration and
adaptive geometry schedules as separate backlog candidates. Do not combine them
with O1 or infer their causal contribution from temporal schedule overlap.
The [Gate 11 backlog](../../RESEARCH_BACKLOG_GATE11.md) records this ordering and
the relevant original source components without changing earlier frozen backlogs.

Freeze a resulting method before independent confirmation on another dense scene
or a prospectively reserved acquisition. No new dataset or method is launched here.
Use the final adjacent freeze and `preparation.json` for exact identities and
validation evidence; native O1 success is intentionally not claimed by CPU tests.
